# Section 10: Trade Examples and Risk Management
> **Source**: *E-book Smart Money SMC* (anonymous, 33 pp., translated), PDF pp. 30–33

*Terminology note: "transactions" = trades; "scrapped/scrap" = broken structure; "login" = entry; "b/w" = break-even (BE). The "FROM" strings in the pp. 31–32 text layer are OCR noise. The charts on pp. 32–33 are missing from the PDF (only captions remain); p. 30's caption boxes are empty.*

## Core Idea
The examples combine everything: **HTF liquidity (double top/SnR) → sweep → LTF structure break → IDM/one-candle entry → target the previous major low**. Risk is managed first: **1–2% per trade on your own account, 0.25–1% on a funded account**, move the stop to break-even only when structure allows, and aim for **1:5–1:10 RR**, not 1:30–50.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **M15 → M1 sell template** (pp. 30–31):
  - How: (1) on M15, identify double liquidity (equal highs/SnR) or a bearish order flow after BOS; (2) wait for the summit sweep; (3) confirm with broken internal structure on M5 ("good confirmation") or a body close back below the swept high; (4) drop to M1 and enter via previous-candle liquidity withdrawal, IDM sweep, or a new OB; (5) target the previous major low.
- **Risk sizing**:
  - Own live account: **1–2% max** per trade. Funded/prop account: **0.25–1%** per trade.
- **Stop management by structure**:
  - How: (1) when the trade moves in your favour, protect the stop first; (2) move SL to BE after the market breaks a recent high/low in your direction — **but not** immediately after a BOS if there is still an IDM or unmitigated blocks near the recent high that price will return to; (3) if there is no internal IDM structure left to be taken (caption wording; the p. 33 example is a short targeting the recent major low), BE is acceptable, and take partial profit when the BE point is far from entry; (4) final target = recent major low/high, or take partial there and leave the rest at BE; (5) when the market takes a new IDM, look for a fresh entry per plan.
- **Realistic RR**: 1:5–1:10 is excellent; social-media 1:30–50 RR expectations are a trap.

## Key Concepts
- **Double liquidity**: equal highs/lows forming a sweep target.
- **External liquidity sweep ≠ BOS**: taking outer liquidity is usually followed by a correction.
- **Correct OB without IDM**: if the OB formed after sweeping a double top, IDM is not needed.
- **Break-even (BE) move**: after a favourable structure break, conditioned on no pending IDM/unmitigated blocks.
- **Partial profit**: bank part at the recent major low/high; leave the rest with SL at BE.
- **Risk-first mindset**: think about risk, not profit; trade only high-probability POIs.

## Mental Models
- "**Protect what you have**" before chasing more.
- Think in probabilities from both sides — no holy-grail strategy.
- Structure tells you when BE is safe: if price still owes an IDM sweep, BE will get tagged.

## Anti-patterns
- **Moving SL to BE right after a BOS** while an IDM/unmitigated block remains nearby.
- **Chasing 1:30–50 RR.**
- **Trading everywhere** instead of only high-probability POIs; emotional trading.
- **Treating an external liquidity sweep as a BOS** — expect a correction instead.

## Worked Example
p. 31, M15: the market builds **double liquidity** at a prior high; the trader waits for the summit to be swept. Price spikes above, then internal structure breaks on M5 ("good confirmation"). After the high is taken, price **closes with a body below** — look for the entry on M1. On M1: price took liquidity and closed below; the first entry is a **previous-candle liquidity withdrawal**. The same caption continues, garbled: "when far from choch you can use … use scrap without interruption" — most likely meaning that once price is far from the CHoCH, BOS-based entries (Section 9, model 6) can be used continuously; treat that reading as uncertain. A second entry comes after the IDM sweep at a fresh OB. Price trends down in steps to the **previous major low target** on M15. p. 32 captions add: "this is the correct OB because price first took liquidity from the double top — IDM not needed here," and "wait for the M1 break and sell to the nearest M5 low."

## Key Takeaways
1. Sweep of HTF liquidity + LTF structure break + LTF entry model = full trade.
2. Risk 1–2% (own) or 0.25–1% (funded) per trade.
3. Move SL to BE only when no IDM/unmitigated block is still pending.
4. Target the previous major low/high; take partials.
5. Aim for 1:5–1:10 RR; patience over frequency.

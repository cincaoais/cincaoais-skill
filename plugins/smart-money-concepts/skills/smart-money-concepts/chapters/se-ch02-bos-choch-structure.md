# Section 2: BOS / CHoCH Structure Display
> **Source**: *E-book Smart Money SMC* (anonymous, 33 pp., translated), PDF pp. 6–9

*Terminology note: "Awakening"/"induce"/"motives" = Inducement (IDM); "scrap structure"/"breakdown" = break of structure; "basic minimum" = main low. The p. 8 diagram carries original Russian captions, translated below.*

## Core Idea
SMC is **structure + liquidity**. A high or low is only a *main* (structural) point after the market has taken its **inducement (IDM)** — the first valid pullback — and a break of that main point counts only on a **full candle-body close**. BOS = continuation; CHoCH = reversal.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **IDM-confirmed structure**: A new high is not a "main high" until price takes out the first pullback (IDM) that formed on the way up; likewise for lows.
  - When to use: before labelling any BOS — no IDM, no valid structure.
  - How: (1) after an up-leg, mark the first valid pullback low below the new high as IDM (X); (2) when price trades below IDM, the high is confirmed as main high; the **lowest point of the retracement that swept the IDM** (the lowest point after the BOS) becomes the new main low (HL), confirmed when the next bullish BOS prints (mirror of "LH = highest point after BOS", p. 9); (3) a later body close above that main high = BOS.
- **IDM carry-over**: If price makes a new higher high *without* taking the IDM, the high is not confirmed; move the IDM to the newest pullback ("IDM is carried over from here to there because a new high is being created").
- **Body-close BOS rule**: In bull or bear markets, a BOS needs a full candle close beyond the main high/low. A wick through it is only **"reversal liquidity"** (a sweep).
  - How (after a sweep): if candle H wicks through the level, the break is only valid when a later candle closes **beyond H's extreme**, not merely beyond the old level.
- **CHoCH via POI**: A trend reversal (CHoCH) happens in two cases: (a) price touches/sweeps an HTF POI (e.g. HTF order block) **and then** breaks the first recent high/low → CHoCH on the lower timeframe; (b) price breaks the **main** high/low (the one that produced the last BOS) → CHoCH even with no POI. If no POI was touched, breaking the first recent high/low is just IDM.

## Key Concepts
- **Main high / main low**: structural swing points confirmed by an IDM sweep.
- **IDM (inducement)**: first valid pullback after a new extreme; its sweep confirms the extreme.
- **BOS**: body close beyond the main high/low in the trend direction (continuation only).
- **CHoCH**: first break against the trend (reversal only); looks like a BOS but is not.
- **Internal structure**: swings between the main high and main low; not traded as BOS.
- **Reversal liquidity**: a wick through a structural level with no body close — a sweep, not a break.
- **LH/LL confirmation**: in a downtrend the LH is "the highest point after BOS"; it's confirmed only after the next bearish BOS.

## Mental Models
- Think of IDM as the market's **receipt**: no receipt (IDM taken) → the high is unpaid for, don't trust it.
- "BOS continues, CHoCH changes" — same shape, opposite meaning; decide by trend context, not by appearance.
- Location decides meaning: breaking the nearest low **without** an HTF POI touch = inducement; **after** an HTF POI touch = LTF CHoCH.

## Anti-patterns
- **Marking BOS on a wick**: a shadow beyond the level is liquidity taken, not structure broken.
- **BOS without IDM**: "not a valid BOS because this structure did not output any IDM".
- **Treating every lower low as structure**: consecutive LH→LL moves that never break the nearest high/IDM count as **one move**.
- **Calling the first low-break a reversal** when no POI was touched — it's an IDM sweep.

## Reference Tables
| Case (bearish examples, p. 6) | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Candle K closes below the previous main low | ✅ BOS |
| Price closes below the previous main low **and** candle H | ✅ BOS |
| Price only wicks below main low / candle A, closes back | ❌ liquidity sweep |
| First attempt sweeps, small pullback, then a later candle closes below | ✅ BOS |
| Price didn't close below the main low and candle N | ❌ (valid only if a body closes below N) |

| Case (bullish, p. 7) | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Candle K closes above previous major high | ✅ |
| H sweeps high; after a small pullback candle A closes above H | ✅ true BOS |
| N closes above the high and the previous candle's high | ✅ |
| K only wicks above the major high | ❌ |
| H sweeps; next candle does not close above H | ❌ |
| N closes above the old high but not above candle A's high | ❌ not reliable |

## Worked Example
p. 8 schematic (Russian captions): an uptrend prints BOS → IDM → BOS. Price then runs into an **HTF order block** ("ордерблок старшего ТФ") and falls. Caption 1: *"Breaking the nearest low is not a trend break but an inducement, if price has not touched the HTF block zone"* (lower-left IDM). Caption 2, at the drop from the HTF OB: *"This is not an inducement but a trend break on the lower timeframe, because price touched the HTF order-block zone"* → marked CHoCH. Caption 3: this also applies if price sweeps the previous high and closes with a body below. Caption 4, in the new downtrend: *"This is not a trend break but an inducement, because the trend is now bearish; breaking the nearest high is not a trend break"* → IDM, followed by a bearish BOS. Far right: a CHoCH with **no POI**, formed by breaking the main low.

## Key Takeaways
1. Validate every high/low with an IDM sweep before using it.
2. BOS and CHoCH need a full body close; wicks are sweeps.
3. After a wick sweep, the confirming close must exceed the sweep candle's extreme.
4. At an HTF POI, the first recent high/low break is an LTF CHoCH; elsewhere it's IDM.
5. Carry the IDM forward when new extremes print without taking it.
6. Internal swings between main high and low are not tradeable structure.

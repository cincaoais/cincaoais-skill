# Glossary — Smart Money Concepts (FM + SE)

`FM` = Frank Miller book (chapter), `SE` = anonymous SMC e-book (section). Alphabetical.

**Advanced CHoCH** — CHoCH on the lower timeframe after price reaches a HTF POI (esp. POI types 3–4); the trigger for entry (FM ch6–7)
**AOI** — Area of interest; synonym of POI (SE s0)
**ATR rule** — if OB candle range < 60% of ATR(14), widen the OB zone symmetrically until its total height = 1 ATR (example: ATR 30 pips, OB < 18 pips → 30-pip zone) (FM ch4)
**Basic POI** — POI type 1: pullback sweeps the previous minor high/low, BOS follows, wait for retrace to it (FM ch6)
**BOS** — Break of structure: body close beyond the last major high (uptrend) / low (downtrend) = continuation. FM also requires a prior liquidity sweep; SE requires the high/low to be IDM-confirmed (FM ch2, SE s2)
**BSL / SSL** — Buy-side liquidity (above highs) / sell-side liquidity (below lows) (SE s0)
**Candle Breakout Strategy (CBS)** — enter from a reaction candle at a POI/OB after an engulfing confirmation candle (FM ch8)
**CHoCH** — Change of character: first close beyond the swing that created the latest BOS, against the trend = possible reversal (FM ch2, SE s2)
**Confirmation candle** — second CBS candle; engulfs the reaction candle's body (FM ch8)
**Continuation OB** — OB formed with the dominant trend; higher win rate (FM ch4)
**Corrective wave / pullback** — counter-trend move; valid only when a candle breaks the extreme candle's opposite end (FM ch1, SE s1)
**D2S / S2D** — Demand-to-supply / supply-to-demand: a failed zone flips polarity (FLiP) (SE s9)
**Decisional OB** — first OB before/after the IDM; first place price may react (SE s4)
**Discount zone** — below the 50% level of the current impulse; buy area in uptrends (FM ch3)
**ENG LQD** — term undefined in SE; standard SMC meaning is engineered liquidity (equal highs/lows built to attract stops). SE's only example (p. 15) is a swept prior high → sell after the candle closes (SE s5)
**EQH / EQL** — Equal highs / equal lows (double/triple tops-bottoms): liquidity pools (FM ch3, SE s6)
**Equilibrium** — the 50% level of the impulse A→B (FM ch3)
**ERL** — SE lists it as "extreme liquidity" without defining it; standard SMC meaning (interp) is external-range liquidity beyond the major swing points (SE s0)
**Extreme OB** — the OB at the very origin of the impulse; target if the decisional OB fails (SE s4)
**FBOS** — False break of structure; SE only lists the term. Interp: a wick/non-closing break that is really a sweep (SE s0)
**FLiP** — Entry module 2: price reacts at an LTF OB, fails, breaks it; the zone flips polarity (SE s9)
**FVG / IMB / Imbalance / Inefficiency (IPA)** — gap between candle 1 and candle 3 wicks around a strong candle 2 (FM ch4, SE s3)
**HTF / LTF** — Higher / lower timeframe; use exactly one of each (FM ch9, SE s8)
**HOD / LOD** — High / low of the day (SE s0)
**IDM (Inducement)** — must be swept to confirm a major high/low and the BOS. FM: the last internal pullback (X) formed before the BOS, confirmed once price closes beyond the major high/low. SE: the first pullback/OB after a BOS, a trap (SMT) (FM ch5, SE s2)
**IFC** — Institutional funding candle: candle that sweeps a major swing, session high/low or IDM but fails to close beyond it; enter on LTF confirmation; colour irrelevant (SE s5)
**Impulsive wave** — strong trend move of large-bodied candles breaking the prior high/low; internal moves without a sweep of internal lows count as one impulse leg (FM ch1, ch5)
**Internal structure** — minor highs/lows inside a major swing (between main high and main low) (FM ch5, SE s2)
**Liquidity** — clustered stop-losses/pending orders the market needs to move (FM ch3)
**Liquidity sweep / grab** — price trading through a high/low where stops sit. FM counts a wick *or* a close through a pullback high/low as a sweep; at a structural (major) high/low a wick without a close = sweep, a close = BOS/CHoCH. SE: a shadow through the level = reversal liquidity (FM ch2–3, SE s2)
**LQD** — Liquidity; used as "no IDM and no LQD" (p. 25), "removed the LQD" (p. 22), "ENG LQD" (p. 15) (SE s0)
**Major (main) high/low** — swing point confirmed by an IDM sweep; the reference for BOS/CHoCH (FM ch5, SE s2)
**Mitigated** — a zone price has already returned to; considered used, single-use (SE: "softened") (FM ch4, SE s4)
**Modified POI** — POI type 2: the first pullbacks didn't sweep; use the lower zone that did (FM ch6)
**OB (Order block)** — FM: last candle that sweeps prior-candle liquidity + followed by imbalance + followed by BOS/CHoCH. SE: liquidity taken + imbalance (FM ch4, SE s4)
**OF (Order flow)** — the last opposite move before an impulse; the "unrefined" OB; single-use once mitigated (SE s4)
**PDH / PDL, PWH / PWL** — Previous day / week high and low (SE s0, s6)
**POI (Point of interest)** — HTF zone needing only a liquidity sweep; where to look for an LTF entry (FM ch6, SE s7)
**Premium zone** — above 50% of the current impulse; sell area in downtrends (FM ch3)
**Reaction candle** — first CBS candle; must touch a POI/OB (FM ch8)
**Reversal OB** — OB formed after a CHoCH against the trend; lower win rate, bigger R:R (FM ch4)
**Session liquidity** — Asia/London/New York highs and lows as liquidity (SE s6)
**Single-candle entry / one-candle formation** — candle takes the previous candle's high and closes bearish (sell) / takes its low and closes bullish (buy) → enter next open (SE s9). In SE p. 22 case 4 the confirmation is instead the *next* candle closing back above
**SMC** — Smart money concepts
**SMT** — "Smart money trap" in SE: the first pullback/OB after a BOS, which acts as inducement. (Elsewhere SMT means "SMT divergence"; not used in these books) (SE s6)
**SnR** — Support and resistance (retail pattern, treated as liquidity) (SE s6)
**SOS / SOW** — Sign of strength / sign of weakness (SE s0, listed only)
**TL** — Trendline; trendline liquidity sits just beyond it (FM ch3, SE s6)
